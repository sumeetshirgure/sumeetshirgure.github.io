![Portrait](/assets/images/pfp.jpg)

I'm a PhD student at University of Central Florida, advised by Prof. Siyuan Niu at "Suica Lab".
My research focus is in Quantum Information Science and Computer Architecture.
Lately I've been focusing on low latency decoder systems for fast fault tolerant quantum computation.
Apart from that we are also researching partially fault tolerant architectures
for quantum simulation of condensed matter Hamiltonians.

I got my Master's in Computer Science with honors from USC,
where I briefly worked under Prof. Massoud Pedram at "SPORT Lab".
I improved VLSI EDA tools for superconducting electronics.

Before that I briefly worked as a software engineer at Google in the call ads department.

I received my undergraduate degree in Computer Science from Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur.
Most notably I participated in ICPC style programming contests.
My teammates and I even ended up representing India at the [2019 World Finals](https://icpc2019.up.pt).
