A funny thing happened while I was walking around Amsterdam.
I had taken photos along a path from my hostel to the city center.
And my phone battery was running low.
So while returning I couldn't run WiFi or look at Google Maps
and had to rely on glancing at the photos for a few seconds.
But the photos were taken from a perspective of the other side of the street.
This motivated a combinatorial problem : "The Amsterdam Walk Problem",
you are at a point on an undirected graph, and you want to reach your home.
You can cross an edge, and find out only after crossing it whether you
took that path earlier. Can you minimize the expected/worse case time to reach home?
Does your initial journey need to be cycle-free?
Can a similar problem be defined for the quantum random walk case?
