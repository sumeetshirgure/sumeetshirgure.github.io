I received great news on my birthday, that
our work "Characterizing and Benchmarking Dynamic Quantum Circuits"
got accepted in [MICRO 2026](https://www.microarch.org/micro59/)!
This is a top tier computer architecture conference.

We built an extension of the well known benchmarking suite
[`SupermarQ`](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.11045).
Our suite works for circuits with dynamic elements
like mid-circuit measurements and feedforward operations.
I named it [`dynamarq`](https://sumeetshirgure.github.io/dynamarq.github.io/).
This has been a work in progress with me contributing since
October 2025. The paper went up in April 2026, and got good
reviews on SciRate too.
I'm super happy to have been a part of this.
I will be going to Athens in November 2026
to present our work. And a photopost is mandatory.
